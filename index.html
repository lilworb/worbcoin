What is Worbcoin?

Worbcoin is a community-driven digital money built for fast, cheap peer-to-peer transactions. Send money anywhere in the world in seconds with almost zero fees. No banks, no borders, no delays.


Built on BNB Smart Chain (BEP20), WORB combines the security of blockchain with the speed needed for everyday payments in Africa and worldwide.
